— Dolce e Pane, in their own words

Dolce e Pane has been part of Rhode Island since 2003. It bakes the way a family bakery is supposed to — small batches, real ingredients, recipes that taste like somebody's grandmother meant them to.

"We strive to give our customers the best and freshest products using local ingredients whenever possible." That is the whole idea, and it has not changed in twenty years.

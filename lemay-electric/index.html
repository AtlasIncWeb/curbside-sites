Residential wiring

New circuits, rewiring, lighting, switches, and outlets — for renovations, additions, and older Rhode Island homes that were never wired for how we live now.

Panel upgrades

If your panel still has fuses, trips when the dryer and the microwave run together, or has no room left for another breaker, it is undersized for the house. We replace it properly and pull the permit.

Whole-home air-cooled standby generator installation, including the transfer switch and the electrical work behind it, so the house comes back on by itself when the street goes dark.
